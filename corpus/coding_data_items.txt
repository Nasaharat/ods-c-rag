Common Coded Data Items in Cancer Abstracting

When an abstractor codes a case, several core data items describe the tumor
itself. Understanding what each item captures is central to the ODS-C role.

Primary site describes where the cancer started, not where it may have spread.
A cancer that begins in the colon and later appears in the liver is still coded
to the colon as its primary site. Site is recorded with a standardized
topography code so that every registry means the same thing by a given value.

Histology describes the type of cell the cancer is made of, based on how the
tissue looks under a microscope. Two cancers in the same organ can behave very
differently depending on their histology, so this item carries a lot of
clinical meaning. Histology is recorded with a morphology code.

Behavior indicates whether a tumor is benign, of uncertain behavior, in situ,
or malignant. In situ means abnormal cells are present but have not invaded
surrounding tissue, while malignant means the cancer has invaded. Behavior is
usually recorded as a single digit attached to the histology information.

Grade describes how abnormal the cancer cells look compared with normal cells
and how quickly they are likely to grow and spread. A low grade looks closer to
normal tissue and tends to grow slowly, while a high grade looks more abnormal
and tends to be more aggressive. Grade is distinct from stage: grade is about
the cells themselves, while stage is about how far the disease has spread.

Laterality records which side of a paired organ the cancer is on, such as the
left or right lung or breast. It only applies to organs that come in pairs, and
coding it for an organ that has no sides is an error.

These items work together. Site and histology together identify what kind of
cancer it is. Behavior and grade add detail about how the cells are acting.
Laterality pins down location for paired organs. Edit checks compare these
items against each other, because certain combinations are impossible. A
histology that cannot occur at a given site, for example, will be flagged for
review before the case is accepted.
